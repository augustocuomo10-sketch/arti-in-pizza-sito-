The story of Arti in Pizza, Como

Two lines, not a novel. Before Como, Vincenzo travelled Italy as a cook. Then he put down roots here, in Via Carloni.

"Before putting down roots in Como, Vincenzo worked as a cook for years across Italy — in Abruzzo, Rome, the Veneto, Amalfi. In 1997 he opened Arti in Pizza in Via Carloni, bringing with him what he had learned in every kitchen: that pizza is made by hand, every day, with no shortcuts — and that hospitality matters as much as the dough."

— this is how we tell it, until we have his exact words
