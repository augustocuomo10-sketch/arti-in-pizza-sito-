Frequently asked questions about our pizzeria in Como

Delivery, bookings, hours and dough: the questions we get most often on the phone, answered once and for all.

📞

How do I order from Arti in Pizza?

By phone, on 031 300809. The same number covers takeaway, home delivery and table bookings. Online ordering is not active yet: it will come as a next step.

🛵

How much does home delivery cost?

€2 on the total bill, not per dish. Dish prices stay exactly as listed on the menu, with no hidden markup.

🥡

Are takeaway prices different from eating in?

No. The price list is the same whether you eat in the dining room or pick up in store.

🍽️

Can I book a table?

Yes, by phone. The dining room has 4 tables for 5 people and 1 table for 2, for 22 seats in total: bookings respect this real availability.

🕐

What are the opening hours?

Lunch from 11:30am to 3pm, Monday to Saturday. Dinner from 6:30pm to 10pm, every day, Sunday included.

📍

Where is the pizzeria?

At Via Carloni 12, 22100 Como (CO), close to the city centre.

🌾

What kind of dough do you use?

Thin or classic base, your choice. The whole-wheat version costs €1 more. The dough ingredients are water, type 00 flour, yeast, oil and salt.

⚠️

How do you handle allergies and intolerances?

For specific allergies or intolerances, please ask at the counter or by phone. We don't publish unverified allergen badges: we prefer to answer case by case.

🍕

How big is the Ruota size pizza?

It is the large size, meant to be shared: enough for up to 4 people. You'll find it in the menu next to the classic price.

🔥

Are the combo deals limited-time offers?

No, they are always available. They are not expiring promotions: they are part of the everyday menu.

Didn't find your answer? Call 031 300809 during opening hours: you'll get us on the line, not a call centre.
